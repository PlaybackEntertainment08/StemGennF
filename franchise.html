how to apply

An 8-step franchise process — clear, structured, no surprises

From your first conversation to your launch day, every stage is mapped — so you know exactly what comes next.

01. Prequalification

After receiving your request for information, one of our franchise representatives will contact you to discuss your experience, financial qualifications, and answer your initial questions.

02. Personal Profile Form

You will receive a personal profile form via email. This provides background information about you and helps us plan further meetings. The form does not obligate you in any way.

03. Programme Review

Through a one-hour webinar, you will receive an in-depth review of our franchise offering, multi-unit business model, business objectives, market opportunity, existing-location performance, growth-through-franchising strategy, and initial market selection discussion.

04. Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD)

We will schedule a call to review the FDD highlights, including: estimated initial investment, applicable fees during franchise operation, an overview of our support services, and a financial-performance summary of currently operating units.

05. Franchise Owner Validation

We will arrange time for you to speak with existing franchise owners or operating managers about any topic of importance to you.

06. Formal Franchise Application

A formal franchise application and personal financial statement is submitted along with your Request for Consideration.

07. Discovery Day

You will be invited to attend a Discovery Day at our headquarters. You will meet our owners and management team in person and have any remaining questions answered.

08. Franchise Approval & Launch

Following Discovery Day, you make your final decision to become a franchise owner, and StemGenn makes a parallel decision on your application. Upon mutual agreement, franchise agreements are prepared and executed — and you begin working with our real-estate team to secure your location.

What we look for in a franchise partner

  • We seek individuals looking to build an enterprise using our multi-unit business model.
  • We offer single-unit and Area Development franchise agreements. Area Development requires a minimum of three centres within a defined market, developed over an agreed timeline.
  • Candidates should have proven business management or ownership experience and a strong commitment to ongoing marketing investment.
  • Medical candidates can fulfil the requirement that each centre be supervised by a licensed MD themselves.
  • Non-medical candidates must hire a Medical Director or qualified MD to oversee operations at each centre.
