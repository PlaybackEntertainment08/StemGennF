r-cap

Regenerative concentrate of active plasma

Endometrium & ovarian rejuvenation: only the beginning of a new age in regenerative medicine.

R-CAP is a regenerative concentrate of growth factors derived from the alpha granules and dense granules of the patient's own platelets. It is used to treat male and female reproductive health and to facilitate the probability of pregnancy in ART/IVF treatments.

The regenerative bioactive peptides and cytokines in R-CAP have no side effects, because the process only accelerates and stimulates the body's natural repair mechanisms — it does not introduce foreign material.

The key to our health and our own regeneration resides within our own body. R-CAP is not a panacea — it is the beginning of a new era in regenerative medicine.
