our medical team

A multidisciplinary panel of regenerative medicine specialists

StemGenn is led by a core team of senior clinicians and supported by an extended panel of associated consultants — spanning regenerative medicine, gynaecology, aesthetics, functional medicine, longevity and wellness disciplines.

Every patient's protocol benefits from this collective expertise: diagnostics are reviewed multidisciplinarily, treatment plans draw on multiple specialities, and complex cases are discussed across the panel.

  • Extended panel of associated consultants
  • Multidisciplinary case-review approach

how our team works

Multidisciplinary care, by design

Case Review Across Specialities

Complex cases are reviewed across our panel — so your treatment plan benefits from gynaecology, regenerative medicine, functional medicine and wellness perspectives rather than a single viewpoint.

One Coordinator, Many Specialists

You're assigned a single point of contact, but draw on the full panel as needed — with no scheduling friction or repeated explanations between visits.

Continuous Clinical Governance

Protocols, diagnostics and patient outcomes are reviewed regularly by the senior team — ensuring practice stays aligned with international regenerative medicine standards.

Education-Linked Practice

Our clinicians teach and publish through StemGenn's Otlu Academy and partner institutions — keeping clinical practice connected to active research and continuing education.
