our goal

High-quality education in Stem Cells and Regenerative Medicine

We are committed to providing high-quality education in the fields of Stem Cells and Regenerative Medicine. Our aim is to increase awareness and understanding of this promising field among students of the Life Sciences — and we created Otlu Academy to make that happen.

Otlu Academy offers education and training to Life Sciences students pursuing B.Tech, M.Tech, and M.Sc in the key areas listed below.

  • Molecular Medicine

our programs

Two programmes — choose your path

You can join Otlu Academy through one of two programmes. Both are designed to deepen your laboratory skills and prepare you for industry roles in regenerative medicine.

6-Month Dissertation Programme

For: B.Tech / M.Tech / M.Sc Life Sciences students completing their final dissertation.

Join Otlu Academy to complete your 6-month dissertation work as a requirement for your degree. You will gain hands-on, industry-specific training in Stem Cell Science, Molecular Medicine, Immunology, Cell Culture, Biotechnology and Embryology.

Learn the emerging field of Stem Cell Therapy and related topics through lectures, and develop practical skills in a well-equipped laboratory with advanced technologies and instruments.

  • 3 structured modules (see curriculum below)
  • Industry-grade laboratory training
  • Bonus: placement opportunity within our company for top performers upon successful completion
Apply Now

Short-Term Training (2 Months)

For: B.Tech / M.Tech / M.Sc / Ph.D Life Sciences students.

A practical-oriented training programme designed to build confidence and add measurable value to your profile. Certification on completion. Suited to students preparing for industry roles, research positions, or further specialisation.

This programme will help you:

  • Build hands-on laboratory confidence
  • Add a recognised certification to your CV
  • Strengthen your candidacy for industry placements
  • Achieve clear career-development objectives

dissertation curriculum

A 3-module structure for the 6-month programme

Modules 1 and 2 are delivered as online lectures; Module 3 is hands-on laboratory training combined with thesis preparation.

Module 1 — Online Lectures

  • Flow Cytometry: Basics and Applications (with video demonstration)
  • Animal Cell Culture
  • DNA Sequencing

Module 2 — Online Lectures

  • Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) Therapy
  • Convalescent Plasma Therapy

Module 3 — Hands-On Training & Thesis

  • Hands-on laboratory training on PRP and Stem Cells
  • Thesis preparation and submission

why train with otlu academy

Industry-grade learning that prepares you for real roles

Industry-Grade Laboratory

Train in a working regenerative medicine lab equipped with the same advanced technologies and instruments used by professionals delivering live clinical care every day.

Practical-First Curriculum

Real hands-on training on PRP, stem cells and core molecular techniques — not just theory. You leave with skills you can demonstrate from day one of any role.

Mentorship By Practising Scientists

Learn directly from researchers working on live clinical regenerative medicine programmes — not adjunct lecturers. Every question is answered with real-world context.

Placement Opportunity

Strong performers may be absorbed into StemGenn upon completion of the dissertation programme — a direct path from training to your first regenerative medicine role.

who should apply

  • M.Tech (Biotechnology / Life Sciences)
  • M.Sc (Biotechnology, Microbiology, Molecular Biology, Biochemistry, Life Sciences)
