the science of healthy ageing

Adding life to your years — not just years to your life

Longevity medicine is the practical application of biological-ageing science — measuring how the body is ageing at a cellular level, identifying what's driving accelerated decline, and intervening early with evidence-led therapies.

Our longevity programmes combine advanced diagnostics, regenerative therapies, hormone & functional medicine, nutrition, recovery and lifestyle protocols — sequenced into structured journeys that compound over time.

  • Biological-age measurement & tracking
  • Personalised regenerative protocols
  • Long-term follow-up & optimisation

our programmes

Three tiers, designed for where you are

Whether you're optimising performance, reversing early signs of biological ageing, or addressing a specific concern — we have a structured pathway for it.

Longevity Foundation

A focused 3–5 day diagnostic-led programme. Comprehensive testing, biological-age assessment and a personalised longevity plan you can begin implementing immediately.

Longevity Intensive

A 7–10 day immersive combining diagnostics, regenerative therapies, IV protocols, hormone work and lifestyle coaching — for measurable shifts in a single visit.

Longevity Membership

An ongoing 12-month programme with quarterly reviews, repeat diagnostics, regenerative top-ups and continuous protocol adjustment as your biology evolves.

the longevity pillars

Six interventions, layered for compounding effect

Biological Diagnostics

Biological-age testing, advanced bloodwork, hormone panels, genomic and gut screening — the foundation of every programme.

Regenerative Therapies

Autologous stem cells, exosomes, PRP, NAD+ and peptide protocols, sequenced to restore cellular function.

Hormonal & Metabolic Health

BHRT, thyroid, insulin and metabolic optimisation — the foundations of energy, mood and body composition.

Nutrition & Supplementation

Diet, fasting protocols and targeted supplementation built around your biomarkers and lifestyle.

Recovery & Biohacking

Hyperbaric oxygen, cryotherapy, red-light, sleep optimisation and stress recovery for nervous-system balance.

Mind-Body & Lifestyle

Movement programming, breathwork, meditation and lifestyle coaching for long-term sustainability.
