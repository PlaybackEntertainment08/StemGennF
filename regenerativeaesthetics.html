how it works

PRP and the body's own healing response

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) is used for the regeneration of facial tissue. PRP is derived from concentrated plasma of the patient's own blood and contains more than 30 growth factors. This regenerative plasma is injected into the face to restore tissue health from within.

During the Bio Facial procedure, a needling technique triggers a natural immune response in the body. This response activates platelets, collagen, elastin, fibroblasts, and growth factors — all of which contribute to facial tissue regeneration.

Six weeks to new collagen

It takes approximately six weeks for new collagen to form. The induction of collagen heals acne scarring and reverses visible signs of ageing.

the healing process

Three biological stages of skin renewal

Bio Facial PRP works in step with your body's natural repair cycle — from initial inflammation through collagen formation to long-term remodelling.

consult our team

01. Inflammation (2–3 days)

Fibroblasts arrive at the injection site and begin breaking down old, unhealthy cells. Fibroblasts also help collagen deposit in the skin, and unhealthy collagen cells are replaced with new, healthy ones.

02. Regeneration (several weeks)

New collagen is actively formed in the skin during this phase. This is the period in which the skin's underlying structure is rebuilt.

03. Remodelling (up to one year)

Collagen production continues, and the newly formed collagen strengthens the skin over time. The result is regenerated, rejuvenated facial tissue with a smooth appearance and a youthful feel.

recommended schedule

3 sessions over 18 months

Dermatologist recommendation

Dermatologists recommend 3 Bio-Facial PRP sessions over the course of 18 months to fully rejuvenate the facial tissues.

hair restoration with prp

Stimulating dormant follicles for stronger regrowth

PRP therapy is equally effective for restoring hair growth in patients experiencing hair thinning and pattern hair loss. The same growth factors that rejuvenate facial skin also stimulate dormant hair follicles, encouraging stronger, denser hair regrowth.

Who is it for?

  • Early-stage male and female pattern hair loss
  • Diffuse thinning across the scalp
  • Hair loss following stress, illness, or post-pregnancy
  • Patients wishing to support recovery after a hair transplant

How the session works

A small sample of the patient's blood is drawn and centrifuged to isolate the platelet-rich plasma. This concentrated PRP — rich in growth factors — is injected into the scalp at the level of the hair follicles. The treatment is performed in-clinic with no downtime.

Recommended course

A series of sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart, followed by maintenance sessions, gives the best long-term outcomes.
