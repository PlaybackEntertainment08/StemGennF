why diagnostics matter

We measure before we treat — always

Effective regenerative and longevity medicine begins with a precise picture of your biology. We use advanced diagnostics and genomic screening to map exactly where you are, what's driving symptoms or accelerated ageing, and how every intervention is performing over time.

No protocol is recommended without diagnostic justification. No programme is run without follow-up measurement. Your data — not assumption — drives your plan.

  • Biological-age & cellular health markers
  • Hormone, metabolic & nutrient panels
  • Genomic, gut & advanced screening

our diagnostic suite

What we measure — and why

A complete diagnostic landscape spanning biological age, hormones, metabolism, genomics, gut and cardiovascular health.

Biological Age Testing

Epigenetic and telomere markers that measure how your body is ageing at a cellular level — independent of your chronological age.

Advanced Bloodwork

Comprehensive panels covering inflammation, oxidative stress, vitamin & nutrient status, kidney, liver and immune markers.

Hormone & Endocrine Panel

Full hormone screening — sex hormones, thyroid, cortisol, insulin — for hormonal balance, fertility and metabolic health.

Genomic Screening

DNA-based testing for genetic predispositions, drug-response profiles, nutrigenomics and personalised longevity strategy.

Gut & Microbiome Analysis

Stool, microbiome and intestinal-permeability testing — foundational for immunity, mood, metabolism and inflammation.

Cardiovascular & Metabolic Risk

Advanced lipid panels, glucose-insulin dynamics, cardiac risk markers and metabolic-syndrome screening.

Oxidative Stress & Toxicity

Oxidative-stress markers, heavy-metal screening and environmental-toxin exposure analysis where indicated.

Body Composition & Fitness

DEXA, BIA and metabolic-rate testing — objective measurement of muscle, fat, bone density and aerobic capacity.

Cognitive & Sleep Diagnostics

Neurocognitive testing, sleep-quality assessment and stress-recovery measurement for brain & nervous-system optimisation.

the diagnostic process

From sample to strategy in four steps

01. Initial Consultation

Clinical review of your history, current concerns and goals — the foundation for choosing the right diagnostic panel.

02. Sample Collection

Blood draws, saliva, stool and any imaging or specialist tests are collected in a single coordinated visit.

03. Multidisciplinary Review

Results are interpreted by our medical team — not just an automated report. Patterns are connected across systems.

04. Personalised Action Plan

A clear, prioritised plan: what to treat first, which protocols, what lifestyle to change and what to re-measure when.
