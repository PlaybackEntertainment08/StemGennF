international patients

Travel for treatment — without the logistics burden

STEMGENN welcomes international patients seeking advanced regenerative therapies and personalised longevity programmes. Our concierge team handles every logistical detail so you can focus on your treatment and recovery.

From the moment you reach out, you're paired with a dedicated patient coordinator who manages your itinerary, accommodation, transfers, appointments and clinical communication — in one continuous handover.

  • Dedicated patient coordinator from day one
  • End-to-end logistics & itinerary management
  • Discreet, multilingual support throughout your stay

our services

What our concierge handles for you

Every international patient receives full logistical and personal support from arrival through to departure.

Visa Assistance

Documentation support, invitation letters and guidance through the medical visa process for India — so paperwork never delays your treatment.

Accommodation Support

Curated partner hotels and serviced apartments near the clinic, matched to your stay duration, budget and recovery requirements.

Concierge Services

Dedicated patient coordinator, interpreter support, dietary arrangements, recreation and any personal request handled by a single point of contact.

Airport Transfers

Private airport pick-up and drop-off, and chauffeured transfers between accommodation, clinic and any appointments throughout your stay.

how it works

From first message to discharge — in 5 steps

01. Initial Enquiry

Reach out by email, phone or the consultation form. Within 24–48 hours, our concierge team responds with a structured intake form.

02. Clinical Pre-Assessment

A video consultation with the clinical team to understand your concerns, review your medical history, and outline likely diagnostics and protocols.

03. Personalised Programme & Quote

You receive a tailored treatment plan, full itinerary, accommodation options and a transparent quote — reviewed line by line.

04. Travel & Stay

Visa support, flight coordination, hotel booking and airport transfer all arranged. You arrive and we take it from there.

05. Treatment, Recovery & Follow-Up

Concierge-managed days at the clinic, structured recovery time, and a continued follow-up plan you take home — with remote check-ins post-discharge.
