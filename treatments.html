our therapies

Regenerative care, built around your biology

StemGenn's treatment portfolio combines cellular therapies, IV protocols, hormone & functional medicine, and advanced wellness modalities — chosen and combined based on each individual's diagnostic profile and personal goals.

Every protocol is delivered by a multidisciplinary clinical team and supported by ongoing measurement, so progress is tracked rather than assumed.

  • Autologous, science-backed protocols
  • Minimally invasive, evidence-led care
  • Personalised to your biology & goals

treatments we offer

A comprehensive regenerative & wellness menu

Explore our core therapies. Each treatment can be delivered as a stand-alone protocol or combined into a longer programme based on your assessment.

Stem Cell Therapy

Autologous stem cell protocols for tissue regeneration, joint repair, fertility support and systemic recovery.

Exosome Therapy

Cell-derived signalling molecules that accelerate repair, reduce inflammation and support tissue communication.

PRP Therapy

Platelet-rich plasma for skin, hair, joint, fertility and reproductive regeneration — using your own blood.

IV Nutrient Therapy

High-dose intravenous nutrients for energy, immunity, hydration and accelerated recovery.

NAD+ Therapy

Cellular energy restoration via intravenous NAD+ — central to mitochondrial function and healthy ageing.

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy

Oxygen-under-pressure protocols supporting recovery, cognition, anti-ageing and post-illness rehabilitation.

Functional Medicine

Root-cause healthcare addressing biological imbalances, chronic disease and metabolic health.

Hormone Optimisation (BHRT)

Bio-identical hormone replacement therapy — targeted at perimenopause, menopause and andropause balance.

Peptide Therapy

Targeted peptide protocols supporting recovery, immune modulation, growth-hormone optimisation and cognition.

Regenerative Aesthetics

Bio-facial rejuvenation, PRP hair restoration and natural anti-ageing protocols — using your own cells.

Fertility & Reproductive Health

Regenerative gynaecology, PRP and BMAC protocols supporting fertility, menopause and intimate wellness.

Biohacking & Recovery

Cryotherapy, red-light, infrared, breathwork and recovery modalities for performance and longevity.

how we choose your protocol

No two patients receive the same plan

Treatments are selected based on your diagnostic profile, history and goals — not from a template menu. Here's how we decide what's right for you.

Comprehensive Diagnostics First

Bloodwork, hormone panels, biological-age markers, gut and genomic screening where indicated — we measure before we treat.

Multidisciplinary Review

Your diagnostic profile is reviewed by specialists across regenerative medicine, functional medicine, and wellness disciplines.

Layered Protocols

Treatments are sequenced and combined — cell therapy with IV support, recovery with biohacking — for compounding benefit.

Ongoing Measurement

Progress is tracked with repeat diagnostics so protocols can be adjusted — not run on autopilot.
